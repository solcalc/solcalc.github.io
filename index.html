Lucky Pharoah's 3x3 Cluster Collect

May 2026

A simple game 3x3 board that uses cluster pays. You don't see too many of those in video slots!

  • Uses cluster pays and 9 individually spinning reels.
  • Trigger the COLLECT feature by getting the COLLECT symbol on the center reel.
  • Hold & Spin feature that can go on forever. Collect jackpot tokens to win 4 different jackpot prizes.
