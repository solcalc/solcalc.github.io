Magic Card Blackjack

March 2024

A spin on classic blackjack that can be added on as an ante bet.

  • Make sure the Magic Bet box is selected to activate the ante bet.
  • First two cards dealt have a chance to be magic cards, giving up to a 10x multiplier on wins.
  • Multipliers on the two cards combine to form up to a 100x multiplier.
